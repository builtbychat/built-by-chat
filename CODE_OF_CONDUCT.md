# Community code of conduct

We want a town where people can experiment without being diminished. Be welcoming, critique ideas rather than people, respect boundaries and moderation calls, avoid harassment and hate speech, and do not expose private information. Do not manipulate votes, impersonate others, or use community spaces for unsolicited promotion.

Report urgent safety issues through the repository’s private Security Advisory flow until the Tiny Signal Club domain contact is verified. Maintainers may remove content or participation when needed to protect the community. Good-faith correction and repair are preferred when safe.
