## Blunt panel notes

**Mira:** “Tiny Scale is the only one that makes the name do work. Don’t throw that away for a cooler object. Steal Margin Note’s editorial lockup attitude and Proof Stack’s ‘piece arrives and seats’ motion. Kill Stamp Seal.”

**Jules:** “I understood Tiny Scale and Margin Note without coaching. Proof Stack felt like software. Wildcard still looks like a logo kit asterisk until you stare. If Tiny becomes the hero, make TINY literally tiny in the lockup so the joke lands on a thumbnail.”

**Rowan:** “At 24 px Tiny Scale’s notch must be bigger than the current study or it dies. Mono must keep the contribution as a second plane or a cut, not a tint. Anything radial loses to Walmart/sparkle memory. Period + notch is safer than ring + gap.”

## Independent synthesis (Cursor)

Panel winner and my pick align: **build on Tiny Scale**, not restart.

Three Round 05 examples to prototype:

1. **Scaled Period** — refined Tiny Scale. Larger notch, clearer seat, lockup where `TINY` is physically smaller and can tuck toward the notch.
2. **Tiny Footnote** — Tiny Scale × Footnote Bracket. Heavy period with a superscript contribution that changes a small step on the body (structure responds).
3. **Margin Period** — Tiny Scale × Margin Note. Period mark + bracket lockup field; TINY acts as the margin annotation in type.

None of these are approved. They earn tabs for human judgment after build.
