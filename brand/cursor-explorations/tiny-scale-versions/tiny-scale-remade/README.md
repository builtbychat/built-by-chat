# Tiny Scale, remade

Status: working identity prototype; not approved and not installed as the public brand.

## The rule

**Tiny is information, not a shape.**

A real contribution appears at a deliberately small scale. The built and verified result appears at a large scale. The contrast is the identity behavior:

```text
ONE VIEWER SIGNAL
ADD A NIGHT MARKET

                         BUILT + TESTED
                         NIGHT MARKET
```

The system does not ask a square, circle, pill, wedge, notch, or mascot to stand in for participation. It shows the actual participation.

## Why this fits Tiny Signal Club

- “Tiny” becomes a visible scale relationship.
- “Signal” is the viewer's real input, written plainly.
- “Club” is demonstrated by shared decisions and results rather than an invented club object.
- Every episode naturally generates the content needed by the identity: idea, vote, build status, test, and result.
- Future projects can use the same input/result behavior without inheriting town imagery.

## Identity components

- Primary identity: typographic Tiny / Signal / Club hierarchy.
- Dynamic identity: small real input → large real result.
- Required-avatar fallback: `SC` monogram with a tiny `TINY` label.
- Project hierarchy: tiny `TINY SIGNAL CLUB PRESENTS` above the large project title.
- Proof hierarchy: tiny input, medium verification state, large result.

## Motion

1. The actual contribution appears small.
2. A short build/test line advances.
3. The result scales into the primary field.
4. The brand signs the outcome without covering it.

Reduced motion shows the final input, verification, and result at once.

## What was rejected

- notched black period plus square;
- signal bead and bowl;
- member pills on a bench;
- wedge and decorative period;
- any fixed shape pretending to mean “viewer contribution.”

## Prototype limitations

The prototype uses system type. Final identity work requires a packaged open-source display face or custom outlined wordmark. The content-driven hierarchy is the concept; the current letterforms are not final.
