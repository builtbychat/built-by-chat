# Moderator brief and chat commands

Site voting is authoritative. Platform and Discord polls may nominate ideas but never replace the final site result.

Suggested commands: `!vote` → live-room URL; `!town` → map; `!schedule` → Thursday 7 PM / Sunday 3 PM CT; `!idea` → moderated submission form; `!rules` → community expectations; `!support` → optional support policy.

Remove harassment, hate speech, doxxing, vote-manipulation instructions, impersonation, repeated spam, and sexual content involving minors immediately. Time out heated personal attacks; invite critique of the idea instead. Escalate credible threats or private-data exposure to Phaenex. Never copy raw private reports into public chat or the repository.
