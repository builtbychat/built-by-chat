# Audience decision policy

The audience chooses among honest, pre-scoped options. Phaenex is responsible for safety, legality, feasibility, accessibility, and keeping the stated episode promise.

## Before a poll

- Explain what the vote controls, what it does not control, when it closes, and whether a tie rule applies.
- Options must be materially distinct, feasible within the stated scope, and reviewed for safety and hidden cost.
- Do not add a favored option late, reveal live identities, sell influence, or quietly change wording after opening.
- Record the poll definition before broadcasting it. Corrections require closing the poll, explaining the error, and opening a new poll ID.

## Finalization

- The immutable site result is authoritative. Platform and Discord polls may nominate only.
- Highest valid count wins. A tie uses a clearly announced runoff; if time does not permit one, defer the decision. The host does not secretly break ties.
- Low turnout does not invalidate a result unless a minimum participation rule was announced before opening.
- A closed result is never edited. Technical invalidation creates a correction record and a replacement poll linked to the original.

## Safety and feasibility stop

A winning result may be paused only for safety, law, platform rules, rights/privacy, accessibility, undisclosed expense, or demonstrated technical infeasibility. Phaenex must explain the category without exposing private moderation data, preserve the original result, and offer the closest safe implementation, a runoff, or deferral. “I prefer something else” is not a valid stop.

## From result to build

Each consequential build record links to the poll snapshot or approved idea, states the interpretation, lists non-goals and acceptance checks, and records any deviation. AI tools receive only the scoped decision as authoritative input; raw chat remains untrusted context. Human review confirms that the shipped result honors the vote.

## Corrections and rollback

If a shipped result is defective, preserve the history, publish a correction, restore the last known-good town snapshot when necessary, and schedule a transparent repair. Rollback fixes implementation; it does not erase the audience decision. Material reinterpretations return to the audience.
