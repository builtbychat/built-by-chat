# Launch completeness audit

`npm run status` is the source of truth for task state. This audit explains the gate catalog used to avoid a “site is built, therefore launch is ready” mistake.

## Product and integrity

Public routes, accessible map, protected vote changes, immutable results, isolated shows, moderation consent, private Studio boundaries, prompt provenance, correction records, and automated migrations/tests must pass. Security headers, abuse bursts, retention cleanup, backup/restore, and remote OTP behavior remain explicit gates.

## Creative and audience contract

Every vote needs feasible neutral choices, closure/tie rules, and a documented authority boundary. Every AI-assisted artifact needs a decision source, template version, bounded context/tools/cost, independent checks, and human disposition. Credits, quotes, and audience claims come only from verified records.

## Live production

Scenes alone are insufficient. Camera/mic/audio routing, captions, readable overlays, local MKV/remux, storage capacity, marker capture, dual-output encoding/upload, separate chats, dropped connections, missing devices, platform outage, and safe ending behavior must be rehearsed and visually inspected.

## Security, privacy, and rights

Account recovery, 2FA, least privilege, secret scanning, CSP/security headers, incident response, retention/deletion, music/font/art/clip rights, sponsor/affiliate disclosures, moderation escalation, and a route for privacy/security reports are launch gates. A real data incident may require professional legal advice.

## Infrastructure and money

Staging precedes production. D1/DO, Access, Turnstile, email, observability, budget alerts, backups, and rollbacks must be tested. Any domain, plugin, relay, model, email, support, or platform charge pauses for the vendor, exact price, tax if known, renewal price, billing period, and explicit approval.

## Publishing and operations

Platform metadata, thumbnails, captions/transcripts, calendar dates/time zones, episode packs, moderation coverage, patch notes, corrections, analytics consent, and post-show backups require named owners and evidence. No announcement is approved until the unlisted rehearsal passes.

## External checkpoints

The blocked tasks in `PROGRESS.json` are intentionally not automated around: account signup/terms, OTP/2FA, purchases, GitHub organization authority, Cloudflare resource creation/deployment, email verification, OBS/platform login, social publishing, sponsorship/support activation, and going live.
