# Purchase approval gate

No purchase is authorized by this repository or its automation. Before buying or enabling any paid service, present Phaenex with:

1. exact product and vendor;
2. exact checkout price, tax, billing cadence, renewal price, and cancellation terms;
3. why it is needed and the free alternative;
4. remaining amount in the $100 first-month cap;
5. an explicit yes/no approval request.
