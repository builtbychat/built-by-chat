# Privacy policy

Effective July 16, 2026.

Tiny Signal Club uses an opaque signed browser identifier to enforce one current selection per poll. It does not expose public voter identities. Network identifiers used for abuse control are keyed-hashed with a rotating daily input; application records never retain raw viewer IP addresses.

Idea submissions store the idea, optional display credit, consent version, moderation status, and pseudonymous browser/network hashes. Cloudflare and broadcast platforms process technical data under their own policies. Moderation and consent audits are restricted to operators. Poll result snapshots and public credits may be retained as permanent town history. Rejected ideas are scheduled for deletion 30 days after moderation; unmoderated ideas expire after 90 days unless a documented safety or legal hold applies. Private prompt/run and audit records follow the schedule in `docs/launch/DATA-RETENTION.md`.

Post-show feedback stores three 1–5 ratings, an optional private note, and a show-scoped keyed hash of the browser identity. It does not store the raw browser identifier or raw IP, and one browser can replace its response for that show. Feedback rows expire after 90 days. Private host workload check-ins retain minute totals and ratings; operator identity and recovery notes are removed after one year.

Requests to access or delete submitted information will use `privacy@tinysignalclub.com` after the domain and routing are verified. Until then, use the repository’s private Security Advisory/contact workflow; the website must not present the future address as active. Because voting identities are intentionally opaque, a voter may not be identifiable after the browser cookie is lost.
