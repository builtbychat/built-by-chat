# Privacy policy

Effective July 16, 2026.

Built by Chat uses an opaque signed browser identifier to enforce one current selection per poll. It does not expose public voter identities. Network identifiers used for abuse control are keyed-hashed with a rotating daily input; application records never retain raw viewer IP addresses.

Idea submissions store the idea, optional display credit, consent version, moderation status, and pseudonymous browser/network hashes. Cloudflare and broadcast platforms process technical data under their own policies. Moderation and consent audits are restricted to operators. Poll result snapshots and public credits may be retained as permanent town history. Rejected ideas are scheduled for deletion 30 days after moderation; unmoderated ideas expire after 90 days unless a documented safety or legal hold applies. Private prompt/run and audit records follow the schedule in `docs/launch/DATA-RETENTION.md`.

Requests to access or delete submitted information can be sent to `privacy@builtbychat.com` after domain mail is activated, with `hello@builtbychat.com` as the published fallback. Because voting identities are intentionally opaque, a voter may not be identifiable after the browser cookie is lost.
